How Bite works

There are no good or bad foods.

Start with a Bite budget shaped around your goals. Add food, see what it is worth, and use your daily and weekly Bites in a way that feels sustainable—not restrictive.

01

Make it yours

A short setup gives you a starting daily budget and weekly flex allowance. You can adjust both whenever you want.

02

Add what you eat

Log a food from your library, create one yourself, or scan a barcode to look up a product and review its Bite estimate.

03

Stay flexible

Daily Bites are your guide; weekly flex gives you extra room. Movement can earn optional bonus Bites from steps, workouts, and active energy.

Built around real life

Food tracking that stays personal.

  • Private by defaultYour Bite data is kept on your device. Bite does not use an account server.
  • Useful scansBarcode scanning helps find product nutrition; Bite calculates an estimate that you can always review.
  • Move for moreConnect Apple Health or Health Connect only if you want activity-based bonus Bites.
  • Share on your termsExchange a progress code with a partner to see their latest shared progress—without merging your data.
