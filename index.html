The Bite editor

Build it like you ate it.

Choose ingredients from the food library, adjust portions, and put your meal together visually. Organize it as Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snacks.

Flexible by design

Your days don’t have to look the same.

Finish within your daily range and you’re broadly on target. If a day lands a little above it, weekly Flex provides breathing room across the week.

The range is a flexible guide—not a strict ceiling, and not an invitation to eat as little as possible.
