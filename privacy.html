Privacy Policy

Bite is local-first and account-free by design. Your food data, profile, settings, and history are stored on your device. Bite does not run an account server.

Information stored on your device

Bite stores the information you add, including profile details, goals, body measurements, food entries, custom foods, preferences, Bite ranges, and progress history. This information is used to run the app and is stored locally in the app’s private storage. You can export a copy of your Bite data or remove it by using the app’s reset option or deleting the app.

Barcode scans

For barcode product lookup, Bite sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information. Open Food Facts processes that request under its own terms and privacy policy.

Health and activity data

Only after you choose to connect it, Bite reads selected activity data, including steps, workout minutes, and active energy, from Apple Health on iOS or Health Connect on Android. Bite uses this data locally to optionally adjust your effective daily Bite range. Activity is compensated conservatively and is not treated as a direct one-for-one addition to your food allowance. Health data is not sent to TelemetryDeck, Open Food Facts, advertisers, or our own server, and it is not sold.

TelemetryDeck

Bite uses the TelemetryDeck SDK on iOS, Android, and macOS for privacy-focused app analytics. The current app integration uses only the SDK’s automatic session, launch, and technical-app signals. Bite does not send custom analytics events or food, barcode, profile, or Health data to TelemetryDeck. TelemetryDeck states that these signals use an anonymized installation identifier and may include technical context such as the app version, operating-system version, device type, language, and region, and that it does not store IP addresses. See TelemetryDeck’s Privacy FAQ and Privacy Policy for its current terms.

Optional iCloud Sync

Bite° Pro users on iOS can choose to enable iCloud Sync. When enabled, Bite sends a backup of app data to the user’s private CloudKit database, with iCloud Drive used as a fallback where available. Apple processes this information under its own privacy terms. Turning the setting off stops future synchronization. You can remove the synced backup from Bite’s connected settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchase status are handled by Apple, Google Play where applicable, and RevenueCat. Bite receives entitlement and purchase-status information needed to unlock Bite° Pro, but not your full payment-card details. No Bite account is required to use the app.

No advertising or sale of data

Bite does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Children, changes, and contact

Bite is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy and will revise the effective date when we do. Questions can be sent to avocadowitharms@gmail.com.
