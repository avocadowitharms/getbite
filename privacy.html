Privacy Policy

Bite is a local-first app. Your food data, profile, settings, and history are stored on your device. Bite does not run an account server.

Information stored on your device

Bite stores the information you add, including profile details, goals, food entries, custom foods, preferences, progress history, and optional food images. This information is used to run the app and is stored locally in the app’s private storage. You can export a copy of your Bite data or remove it by using the app’s reset option or deleting the app.

Photos, food recognition, and barcode scans

When you choose a food photo, barcode scan, or food-recognition feature, the image analysis needed for the scan runs on your device. Bite does not upload scan photos. If you add a photo to one of your own foods, Bite stores that photo locally with your app data. For barcode product lookup, Bite sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information; Open Food Facts processes that request under its own terms and privacy policy.

Health data

Only after you choose to connect it, Bite reads selected activity data—steps, workout minutes, and active energy—from Apple Health on iOS or Health Connect on Android. Bite uses this data locally to calculate optional activity bonus Bites. Health data is not sent to TelemetryDeck, Open Food Facts, advertisers, or our own server, and it is not sold.

TelemetryDeck

Bite uses the TelemetryDeck SDK on iOS, Android, and macOS for privacy-focused app analytics. The current app integration uses only the SDK’s automatic session, launch, and technical-app signals; Bite does not send custom analytics events or food, barcode, image, profile, or Health data to TelemetryDeck. TelemetryDeck states that these signals use an anonymized installation identifier and may include technical context such as the app version, operating-system version, device type, language, and region; it does not store IP addresses. See TelemetryDeck’s Privacy FAQ and Privacy Policy for its current terms.

Optional iCloud Sync

Bite° Pro users on iOS can choose to enable iCloud Sync. When enabled, Bite sends a backup of app data to the user’s private CloudKit database, with iCloud Drive used as a fallback where available. Apple processes this information under its own privacy terms. Turning the setting off stops future synchronization; you can remove the synced backup from Bite’s connected settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchase status are handled by Apple, Google Play where applicable, and RevenueCat. Bite receives entitlement and purchase-status information needed to unlock Bite° Pro, but not your full payment-card details.

Sharing

Bite does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. If you create and share a partner progress code, you choose to give that code to your partner. They can use it to view the progress represented by that code; Bite does not create a shared account or automatically synchronize partner data.

Children, changes, and contact

Bite is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy and will revise the effective date when we do. Questions can be sent to avocadowitharms@gmail.com.
