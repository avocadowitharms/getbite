Privacy Policy

Bite is designed to keep your food and health information private. Core app data is stored locally unless you choose to enable iCloud Sync.

Information you provide

Bite may store profile details, food entries, food images, goals, preferences, and other information you add. This information is used to provide tracking, estimates, history, and personalization.

Health data

If you grant permission, Bite reads selected Apple Health information such as steps, workouts, and active energy to calculate activity-related features. Health data is not used for advertising and is not sold. You can revoke access in Apple Health or iOS Settings.

Food scanning

Supported food and nutrition-label analysis runs on your device. Results are estimates and may be incorrect. Review information before relying on it.

iCloud Sync

Bite° Pro users may optionally sync app data through their private Apple CloudKit database. Apple processes this information under its own privacy terms. Turning sync off stops future syncing on that device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchase status are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. Bite receives purchase and entitlement information but not your full payment-card details.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate features you choose, when legally required, or with your direction.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you edit it, reset the app, or remove the app. Synced information may remain in your private iCloud account. Contact us if you need assistance with deletion.

Children

Bite is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the service if local law does not permit you to provide the required information.
