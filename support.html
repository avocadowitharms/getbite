A barcode scan cannot find a product

Make sure the barcode is clear, well lit, and fully visible. Bite uses the barcode to look up product information, so some products may not be in the available database. You can always create a food manually and enter its Bite value or nutrition details yourself.

A Bite value looks incorrect

Check the food, portion, and nutritional information before saving. You can choose another item from the food library or add a food manually.

Restore Bite° Pro

Open Bite Settings and select Restore Purchases. Make sure the device uses the same Apple Account or Google account that made the original purchase.

iCloud Sync is not working

iCloud Sync is an iOS Bite° Pro feature. Confirm that Bite° Pro is active, that you are signed in to iCloud, and that iCloud Drive is enabled in iOS Settings. Then open Bite Settings → Connected and select Sync now.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Use your App Store or Google Play subscription settings for Bite. The store manages billing, cancellations, and refund requests.

Export or delete local data

Use Bite Settings to copy a JSON backup before resetting the app. To delete local data, use Bite’s reset option or remove the app. If iCloud Sync was enabled, use Bite’s connected settings to remove the synced backup as well.
