Food scans look incorrect

Food recognition is an estimate. Use the review screen to choose an alternative, search the complete food library, or add a custom food. Clear, well-lit overhead photos with separated foods work best.

Restore Bite° Pro

Open Bite Settings and select Restore Purchases. Make sure the device uses the same Apple Account that originally purchased the subscription.

iCloud is not syncing

Confirm that you are signed into iCloud, iCloud Drive is enabled, Bite° Pro is active, and iCloud Sync is enabled under Bite Settings → Connected. Then select Sync now.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bite. Apple manages billing, cancellations, and refund requests.

Delete local data

Use Bite’s reset option in Settings. Removing the app also removes local app data. If iCloud Sync was enabled, contact support for help removing the private cloud backup.
