How does my Bite Range work?

Your daily Bite Range is a flexible guide based on information such as your body measurements, activity level, and selected goal. It is a range rather than one exact number so normal day-to-day variation has room to exist. Bite can also show the approximate energy equivalent behind your assigned range in Settings.

Why did activity change my range?

If you connect Apple Health or Health Connect, Bite can use selected activity data to adjust your effective daily range. Activity is compensated conservatively rather than being added back one-for-one, and the adjustment is limited. Your base range remains available as part of Bite’s range calculation and history.

A barcode scan cannot find a product

Make sure the barcode is clear, well lit, and fully visible. Bite uses the barcode to look up product information, so some products may not be in the available database. You can create a food manually by entering its nutritional information, and Bite will calculate its Bite value.

A Bite value looks incorrect

Check the food, portion, and nutritional information before saving. Bite values are calculated from nutritional information, so an incorrect serving size or nutrition entry can affect the result. You can choose another item from the food library or correct the food information where available.

Restore Bite° Pro

Open Bite Settings and select Restore Purchases. Make sure the device uses the same Apple Account or Google account that made the original purchase. Bite itself does not require an account.

iCloud Sync is not working

iCloud Sync is an iOS Bite° Pro feature. Confirm that Bite° Pro is active, that you are signed in to iCloud, and that iCloud Drive is enabled in iOS Settings. Then open Bite Settings → Connected and select Sync now.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Use your App Store or Google Play subscription settings for Bite. The store manages billing, cancellations, and refund requests.

Export or delete your data

Use Bite Settings to copy a JSON backup before resetting the app. To permanently delete local entries and customizations, use Bite’s reset option or remove the app. If iCloud Sync was enabled, use Bite’s connected settings to remove the synced backup as well.
