How does Bite work?

Bite is a personal food tracker built around a personalized daily Bite range and weekly Flex allowance. You build meals visually from foods and ingredients, organize them as Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snacks, and can scan a barcode to retrieve product nutrition. Bite turns a food’s nutritional profile into a simpler Bite value. Your daily range is personalized using information such as your body measurements, activity level, and selected goal. The range is a flexible guide rather than a strict maximum or a target to stay below. Bite may also show an approximate energy equivalent to help explain what the assigned range represents. You must review entries, portions, barcode results, and estimates before saving them.

Activity

If you connect Apple Health or Health Connect, Bite can use selected activity information, including steps, workout minutes, and active energy, to optionally adjust your effective daily Bite range. Bite does not simply add all recorded active energy back into your range. Activity compensation is intentionally conservative, uses diminishing returns and limits, and may differ depending on the selected goal. Activity data and resulting adjustments are estimates and may not reflect your actual energy expenditure.
