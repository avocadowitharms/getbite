How does Bite work?

Bite is a personal food-Bites tracker. A short setup creates a suggested daily Bite budget and weekly flex allowance from the information and goals you provide; both can be changed in the app. You log foods from your library, create custom foods, or scan a barcode to retrieve product nutrition and create a Bite estimate. Bite values use energy as the primary measure, with a limited adjustment for protein, fibre, saturated fat, and sugar. You must review entries, portions, scanned results, and estimates before saving them.

Activity and partner features

If you connect Apple Health or Health Connect, Bite can use steps, workout minutes, and active energy to calculate optional activity bonus Bites. You may enable, change, or disable each activity reward in Settings. A partner progress code is a manual, read-only sharing feature: exchanging a code lets the recipient see the progress contained in that code, without a shared account or automatic live sync.
