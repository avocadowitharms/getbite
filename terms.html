How does Bite work?

Bite is a personal food tracker built around a personalized daily Bite range and weekly Flex allowance. You build meals visually from foods and ingredients, organize them as Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snacks, and can scan a barcode to retrieve product nutrition. Bite turns a food’s nutritional profile into a simpler Bite value. The range is a flexible guide rather than a strict maximum. You must review entries, portions, barcode results, and estimates before saving them.

Activity

If you connect Apple Health or Health Connect, Bite can use steps, workout minutes, and active energy to calculate optional activity bonus Bites as part of its flexible range system. You may enable, change, or disable each activity reward in Settings.
